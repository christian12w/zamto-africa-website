Vehicle Description

This exceptional 2020 Toyota Land Cruiser 200 Series URJ202 represents the pinnacle of luxury SUV engineering. With an incredibly low mileage of only 32,400 km, this vehicle is in pristine condition and has been meticulously maintained.

Powered by Toyota's legendary 1UR-FE 4.6L V8 engine producing approximately 310 horsepower, this Land Cruiser delivers effortless power and smooth acceleration. The full-time 4WD system with locking differentials ensures capability in any terrain, from Zambian roads to remote wilderness.

The White exterior conceals a luxurious 8-seat interior appointed with premium materials and modern amenities. Features include leather upholstery, dual-zone climate control, navigation system, and comprehensive safety systems including multiple SRS airbags and ABS braking.

Imported directly from Japan, this right-hand drive Land Cruiser 200 Series is ready for immediate delivery. Ideal for executives, families, or anyone seeking the ultimate combination of luxury, capability, and reliability.
