Vehicle Description

This impressive 2023 Toyota Hilux Double Cab represents the pinnacle of pickup truck engineering. Finished in sleek Black with aggressive styling cues, this Hilux commands attention on any road.

Powered by Toyota's renowned 2.8L GD-6 turbo-diesel engine producing approximately 201 horsepower and 500 Nm of torque, this Hilux delivers exceptional performance for both work and recreation. The automatic transmission ensures smooth shifts while maximizing fuel efficiency.

With 4x4 capability, LED lighting, bull bar, side steps, and roof rails, this vehicle is ready for any adventure. The double cab configuration provides comfortable seating for five with an enclosed load bed perfect for cargo or equipment.
